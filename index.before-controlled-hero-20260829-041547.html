About Huddle Up N Travel

Travel is better
when we huddle up.

Travel has always been more than a vacation to us—it is an opportunity to experience the beauty, diversity, and wonder of God's creation.

Since childhood, I have dreamed of exploring the world. After raising our children into adulthood, my wife and I decided it was time to pursue that dream together, and Huddle Up N Travel was born.

With a lifelong passion for serving others, mentoring families, and helping people create meaningful experiences, we believe that travel brings people closer together.

Whether it is a family reunion, couples' getaway, destination wedding, church retreat, group cruise, or the vacation you've always dreamed about, we are committed to making your journey enjoyable from beginning to end.

Dream. Discover. Go Together. We don't simply book trips—we help create memories that last a lifetime.
Nick C. Henriques
Founder, Huddle Up N Travel
